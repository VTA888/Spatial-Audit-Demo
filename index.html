Spatial-Audit Audio Editing Demo

Qualitative examples for spatial-semantic audio editing with a spatially adapted TangoFlux backbone and training-free FlowEdit.

Use headphones. All examples are 10-second binaural clips. Captions describe locations with phrases such as from the left, from the front, and from the right.

Evaluation settings

This demo addresses three evaluation settings described in the revised manuscript: synthetic HRTF in-domain data, independent real-world MRSAudio recordings, and randomized temporal overlap conditions.

1. In-domain HRTF data

Each case includes the source, target/reference, Spatial-Audit, AudioEditor, MMEdit, and SmartDJ outputs.

2. Real-world MRSAudio

These single-source binaural recordings are independent of the AudioCaps/HRTF synthesis pipeline. They evaluate spatial addition without a synthetic ground-truth target.

3. Diverse temporal overlap

These examples use randomized event onset times and overlap durations. The replacement example contains approximately 3.2–7.7 seconds of source-event overlap, and the removal example approximately 0.8–8.2 seconds.

Notes

  • Spatial-Audit editing is training-free and uses source/target spatial-semantic captions.
  • SmartDJ prompts explicitly include the edited event direction.
  • Real-recorded examples have no synthetic target; listen to Spatial-Audit and SmartDJ directly.
