Randomized Temporal Overlap Conditions

Examples with randomized event onset times and overlap durations. The replacement case overlaps at approximately 3.2–7.7 s, while the removal case overlaps at approximately 0.8–8.2 s.

Spatial-Audit uses a spatially adapted TangoFlux backbone with inversion-free FlowEdit. The editing stage is training-free.
